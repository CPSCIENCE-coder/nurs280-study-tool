# NURS 280 — Human Sexual Health Study Tool

A free, browser-based study aid for the UBC **NURS 280 (Human Sexual Health)** midterm.
Built from the course lecture notes, recorded lecture transcripts, the Rathus textbook
*Human Sexuality in a World of Diversity*, the Basson (2001) reading, and the instructor's
"links to explore."

## 🔗 Live site
Once published with GitHub Pages, your tool will be at:
`https://<your-username>.github.io/<your-repo-name>/`

## What's included
- **`index.html`** — the study tool: Study Notes, Flashcards, Practice Quiz, full-text
  Search (across notes, the 580-page textbook, lecture transcripts, the Basson reading,
  and linked articles), and Resources. Works fully offline; no login or install.
- **`cram-sheet.pdf`** — a printable one-page, high-yield review of all 10 lectures.
- **`ai-tutor.html`** — an "ask anything" + image-question tutor. *Note:* its AI-generated
  answers run inside the Claude/Cowork app; on a plain website the Search tab in
  `index.html` is the offline equivalent.

## How to use
Open the live link (or download and double-click `index.html`). Pick a tab and study.

## Disclaimer
A study aid for preparation and self-testing — not for use during the exam. Verify against
your own notes and official course materials. Textbook passages were extracted via OCR and
may contain minor character errors.
